From rough stone to finished piece, in five stages

01

Sourcing

Every stone is bought directly from small-scale cutters we've worked with for years, with full chain of custody.

02

Sketching

The setting is drawn around that specific stone's shape, not adapted from a stock template.

03

Casting

Recycled 18k gold is cast in small batches of under twenty pieces at a time.

04

Setting

Our bench jeweller sets each stone by hand, checking the angle of light before it's ever polished.

05

Finishing

A final hand-polish and inspection, then it's packed and ready to travel to you.
